# Competitor Alternatives Skill

Create "X alternatives" or "[Competitor] vs. [Your Brand]" comparison content.

## Content Types

### "[Competitor] Alternatives" Page
High-intent content capturing competitor-branded searches.

Structure:
1. Why people look for alternatives (pain points)
2. What to look for in an alternative
3. [Your product] as the #1 alternative (detailed)
4. Other alternatives (brief, honest)
5. How to choose
6. FAQ

### "[Competitor] vs. [Brand]" Comparison
Balanced comparison that positions your product fairly but favorably.

Structure:
1. Overview of both products
2. Feature comparison table
3. Pricing comparison
4. Who each product is best for
5. Your product's key advantages
6. Conclusion and recommendation

### "Best [Category] Tools" Roundup
Broader comparison that includes you as a top option.

## Usage

Provide:
1. Competitor name(s)
2. Your product name
3. Key differentiators and advantages
4. Target audience
5. Content type (alternatives, comparison, roundup)

## Output

Full article or page draft optimized for competitor-branded keywords, maintaining honest positioning while clearly demonstrating your product's advantages for the right audience.
