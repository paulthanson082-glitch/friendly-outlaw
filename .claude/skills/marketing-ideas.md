# Marketing Ideas Skill

Generate creative marketing campaign ideas, content concepts, and growth tactics.

## Idea Categories

### Content Marketing
- Original research and data studies
- Free tools and calculators
- Ultimate guides and hubs
- Interview series
- Community-sourced content

### SEO Growth
- Programmatic content at scale
- Competitor alternative pages
- Topic cluster domination
- Featured snippet targeting
- Long-tail keyword content sprints

### Partnership & Distribution
- Co-marketing with complementary brands
- Guest content and podcast appearances
- Newsletter sponsorships
- Integration marketplace content

### Community & Social Proof
- User-generated content campaigns
- Customer success spotlights
- Community-built resources
- Ambassador programs

### Conversion & Retention
- Free tool as lead magnet
- Email course sequences
- Webinar series
- Resource library

## Usage

Provide:
1. Business type and product
2. Target audience
3. Goal: traffic / leads / brand / retention
4. Budget or resource level: bootstrap / mid / enterprise
5. Channels available

## Output

- 10-15 specific, actionable campaign ideas
- Prioritized by expected impact and effort
- Implementation outline for top 3 ideas
- Success metrics for each
