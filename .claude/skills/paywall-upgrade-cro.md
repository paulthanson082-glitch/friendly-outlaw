# Paywall & Upgrade CRO Skill

Optimize freemium paywalls, upgrade prompts, and paid conversion flows.

## Upgrade Trigger Types

### Feature Limit Hit
User reaches the cap of a free feature.
- Best approach: show value of paid, not just the wall
- Copy: "You've used X of Y [feature]. Upgrade to unlock unlimited."

### Usage Limit Hit
User hits a quota (storage, API calls, seats, etc.).
- Best approach: contextualize the limit and the upgrade benefit
- Copy: specific numbers and what unlocks

### Feature Discovery
User tries to access a paid-only feature.
- Best approach: show a preview, explain the upgrade
- Copy: "This is a Pro feature. Here's what you get..."

### Trial Expiry
Free trial is ending.
- Best approach: remind of value experienced, create urgency
- Copy: "Your trial ends in X days. Keep access to [specific features]."

## Paywall Copy Framework

1. **Acknowledge the moment**: don't just say "Upgrade now"
2. **Show what they get**: specific features and benefits
3. **Address price objection**: anchor to value, not cost
4. **Risk reversal**: cancel anytime, money-back guarantee
5. **CTA**: clear, action-oriented

## Upgrade Email Sequence

- Email 1: "You've been getting value" — recap their usage
- Email 2: Feature spotlight — what paid unlocks
- Email 3: Social proof — success story from paid user
- Email 4: Urgency — trial ending / offer expiring
- Email 5: Last chance + support offer

## Usage

Provide:
1. Trigger type (feature limit, usage limit, trial, etc.)
2. Paid plan benefits vs. free
3. Pricing (or at least pricing tier names)
4. Current upgrade conversion rate (if known)

## Output

- Paywall/modal copy
- Upgrade email sequence
- CTA options
- Objection handling copy
- A/B test recommendations
