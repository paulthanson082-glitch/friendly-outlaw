# Marketing Psychology Skill

Apply behavioral psychology principles to improve copy, design decisions, and conversion strategy.

## Key Principles

### Scarcity & Urgency
- Limited time offers, countdown timers
- Limited availability ("Only 3 spots left")
- Best for: pricing pages, checkout, email

### Social Proof
- Numbers: "Join 50,000 creators"
- Testimonials: specific, named, with outcomes
- Authority: logos, press, awards
- Best for: landing pages, pricing, checkout

### Loss Aversion
- Frame as what users lose by NOT acting
- "Don't miss out on [benefit]"
- Best for: email subject lines, CTAs, pricing

### Reciprocity
- Give value before asking
- Free tools, guides, templates
- Best for: lead magnets, email nurture

### Commitment & Consistency
- Small yes leads to bigger yes
- Free trial → paid conversion
- Best for: onboarding, upgrade prompts

### Anchoring
- Show higher price first
- Before/after transformation
- Best for: pricing pages, sales copy

### Cognitive Ease
- Simple > complex
- Familiar patterns
- Best for: UX copy, instructions, onboarding

## Usage

Provide:
1. Page or copy element to optimize
2. Conversion goal
3. Known objections or friction points
4. Current copy (if optimizing existing)

## Output

- Specific psychological principles to apply
- Before/after copy examples
- Implementation priority
- Expected psychological impact explanation
