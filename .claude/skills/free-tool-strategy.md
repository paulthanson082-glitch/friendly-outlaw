# Free Tool Strategy Skill

Plan and develop free tool concepts as SEO and lead generation assets.

## Why Free Tools Work

- Earn links naturally (people link to useful tools)
- Rank for high-volume, low-competition keywords
- Capture leads via email gate or signup
- Build brand awareness and trust
- High shareability on social

## Tool Types by Effort

### Low Effort (calculators, generators)
- Word count calculator
- Reading time estimator
- Pricing calculator
- Name/idea generator
- ROI calculator

### Medium Effort (templates, assessments)
- Downloadable template packs
- Quizzes and assessments
- Comparison tools
- Checklist builders

### High Effort (interactive tools)
- Full-featured free plan of your product
- Industry benchmark tools
- Data visualization tools

## Strategy Components

### Keyword Targeting
- Head term: "[X] calculator" or "[X] generator"
- High volume, transactional intent
- Lower competition than blog content

### Lead Capture
- Email gate before results (high friction, more leads)
- Results first, gate for advanced features (low friction, more users)
- No gate, but prominent signup CTA

### Promotion
- ProductHunt launch
- Reddit/community posts
- Newsletter mention
- SEO for tool-specific keywords

## Usage

Provide:
1. Industry and product
2. Target audience
3. SEO goal vs. lead generation goal priority
4. Development resources available

## Output

- 5-10 tool concepts with keyword opportunity assessment
- Recommended tool to build first
- Feature scope for MVP
- SEO keyword strategy for the tool
- Lead capture recommendation
- Promotion plan
