# Launch Strategy Skill

Plan content and marketing strategy for product launches, feature releases, or campaigns.

## Launch Types

### Product Launch
Full new product introduction:
- Pre-launch: anticipation and waitlist building
- Launch day: announcement and activation
- Post-launch: adoption and retention content

### Feature Launch
New feature announcement:
- Blog post: feature deep-dive
- Email: user announcement
- In-app: contextual discovery
- Social: awareness

### Content Campaign Launch
Coordinated multi-channel content push:
- Anchor piece (guide, research, tool)
- Supporting content
- Promotion plan

## Content Planning

### Pre-Launch (2-4 weeks)
- Teaser content and building anticipation
- SEO content positioning around launch topic
- Email list segmentation
- Partner and influencer outreach

### Launch Day
- Announcement blog post
- Email blast to full list
- Social posts across platforms
- PR outreach

### Post-Launch (2-4 weeks)
- Use case content
- Tutorial and how-to content
- Customer success stories
- Performance analysis

## Usage

Provide:
1. Launch type and timeline
2. What's being launched
3. Target audience
4. Available channels
5. Success metrics

## Output

- Launch content calendar
- Copy for each channel
- Sequencing and timing recommendations
- Measurement plan
