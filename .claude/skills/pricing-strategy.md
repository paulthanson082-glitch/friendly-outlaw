# Pricing Strategy Skill

Analyze and optimize pricing presentation, page copy, and packaging for conversion.

## Analysis Areas

### Pricing Page Audit
- Plan differentiation and naming
- Feature comparison clarity
- Anchor pricing (enterprise/most expensive shown first?)
- Recommended plan highlighting
- Social proof on pricing page
- FAQ and objection handling
- CTA clarity per plan

### Pricing Psychology
- Charm pricing ($99 vs. $100)
- Decoy effect (3 plans with middle as target)
- Loss aversion framing ("Save $X" vs. "Pay $X")
- Anchoring (show higher value first)
- Scarcity and urgency elements

### Packaging Optimization
- Feature allocation by tier
- Good/better/best structure
- Freemium vs. free trial considerations
- Annual vs. monthly discount positioning

## Usage

Provide:
1. Current pricing page URL or file
2. Current conversion rate (if known)
3. Target plan to promote
4. Main objection to price (too high? unclear value? etc.)

## Output

- Pricing page critique with specific improvements
- Copy alternatives for plan names, descriptions, CTAs
- Psychological optimization opportunities
- A/B test recommendations
