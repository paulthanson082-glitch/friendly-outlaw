# Email Sequence Skill

Design and write multi-email sequences for onboarding, nurture, re-engagement, or sales.

## Sequence Types

### Welcome / Onboarding (3-7 emails)
- Email 1: Welcome + quick win
- Email 2: Value delivery + social proof
- Email 3: Feature spotlight + use case
- Email 4: Case study or success story
- Email 5: Upgrade or engagement CTA

### Nurture Sequence (4-8 emails)
- Educational content leading to soft CTA
- Builds trust before asking for conversion

### Sales / Launch Sequence (5-10 emails)
- Announcement → Education → Social proof → Objection handling → Urgency → Close

### Re-engagement (3-5 emails)
- Win-back subscribers who've gone cold

### Drip / Course (variable)
- Lesson-based content delivery

## Usage

Provide:
1. Sequence type
2. Audience (segment and where they are in the funnel)
3. Goal (what action should they take at the end?)
4. Product/service being promoted
5. Number of emails
6. Sending cadence

## Output

For each email:
- Subject line (3 variations)
- Preview text
- Full email body
- CTA
- Notes on purpose and position in sequence
