# Content Strategy Skill

Build a content strategy framework for a specific goal, audience, or time period.

## Strategy Types

### Topic Cluster Strategy
Build topical authority around pillar keywords:
- Define pillar pages
- Map supporting cluster content
- Internal linking architecture
- Publishing sequence

### Funnel-Based Strategy
Align content to buyer journey stages:
- TOFU (awareness): educational, problem-aware content
- MOFU (consideration): comparison, solution-aware content
- BOFU (decision): case studies, feature content, conversion content

### Competitive Gap Strategy
Beat competitors by covering what they miss:
- Competitor content audit
- Gap identification
- Differentiation angles

### Brand Authority Strategy
Build thought leadership:
- Opinion pieces and POVs
- Original research content
- Expert interview content

## Usage

Provide:
1. Primary business goal (traffic, leads, brand authority, etc.)
2. Time horizon (30/60/90 days or 6/12 months)
3. Resources (posts per month, team size)
4. Current content inventory

## Output

- Strategy recommendation with rationale
- Content calendar framework
- Priority content types
- KPIs to track
- Quick wins to start immediately
