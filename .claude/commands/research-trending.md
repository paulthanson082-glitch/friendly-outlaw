# /research-trending

Identify trending topic opportunities in your industry for timely content creation.

## Usage
`/research-trending`

## What This Command Does

1. **Industry Trends**: identify emerging topics and rising searches in your space
2. **Seasonal Opportunities**: upcoming seasonal or event-based content opportunities
3. **News & Events**: recent industry news that could anchor content
4. **Rising Keywords**: terms gaining search volume momentum
5. **Content Ideas**: specific article ideas based on trends

## Output

Save to: `research/trending-[YYYY-MM-DD].md`

## Instructions

Identify 5-10 trending content opportunities relevant to the industry and topics in `context/target-keywords.md`. For each, explain the trend, estimate the opportunity window, and suggest a specific article angle. Focus on trends that align with brand positioning in `context/brand-voice.md`.
