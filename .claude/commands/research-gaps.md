# /research-gaps

Identify content gap opportunities by analyzing competitor content vs. your published content.

## Usage
`/research-gaps`

## What This Command Does

1. **Inventory Your Content**: list all topics covered in `published/` and `drafts/`
2. **Competitor Content Audit**: review competitors from `context/competitor-analysis.md`
3. **Gap Identification**: topics competitors rank for that you don't cover
4. **Opportunity Assessment**: for each gap, evaluate search volume, difficulty, and business value
5. **Prioritized Gap List**: ranked list of highest-opportunity content gaps

## Output

Save to: `research/content-gaps-[YYYY-MM-DD].md`

## Instructions

Compare your content inventory against competitors listed in `context/competitor-analysis.md`. Identify the most valuable content gaps and provide a prioritized list of topics to create. Focus on gaps where you have a genuine advantage or unique angle.
