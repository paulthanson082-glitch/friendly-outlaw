# /landing-research

Research competitors and positioning for a landing page topic.

## Usage
`/landing-research [topic/product category]`

## What This Command Does

1. **Competitor Landing Pages**: identify and analyze 5-7 competitor pages
2. **Headline Analysis**: catalog headline formulas competitors use
3. **Value Proposition Patterns**: common positioning angles in the category
4. **Differentiation Opportunities**: what competitors miss or do poorly
5. **CTA Patterns**: what CTAs and offers are common
6. **Social Proof Benchmarks**: what trust signals competitors use

## Output

Save to: `research/landing-research-[topic-slug]-[YYYY-MM-DD].md`

## Instructions

Research the competitive landscape for "$ARGUMENTS" landing pages. Identify patterns, gaps, and differentiation opportunities. Provide a positioning brief that will guide writing a landing page that stands out and converts better than competitors.
