# /landing-competitor

Deep analysis of a competitor's landing page for positioning and CRO insights.

## Usage
`/landing-competitor [URL]`

## What This Command Does

1. **Full Page Teardown**
   - Above-the-fold breakdown
   - Headline formula and value proposition
   - Copy tone and persona targeting
   - Offer structure (trial, demo, freemium)

2. **CRO Analysis**
   - CTA placement and copy
   - Trust signal inventory
   - Objection handling tactics
   - Social proof types used

3. **SEO Analysis**
   - Title and meta description
   - H1 and heading structure
   - Target keyword strategy

4. **Strengths & Weaknesses**
   - What they do well (don't copy, learn from)
   - Gaps and weaknesses (your opportunity)
   - Positioning vs. your brand

5. **Actionable Takeaways**
   - What to incorporate in your own page
   - How to differentiate

## Output

Save to: `research/competitor-lp-[domain]-[YYYY-MM-DD].md`

## Instructions

Perform a thorough teardown of the landing page at "$ARGUMENTS". Be analytical and strategic — identify what makes it effective and where it falls short. Translate findings into concrete recommendations for your own landing page.
