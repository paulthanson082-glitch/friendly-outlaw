# CRO Best Practices

Standards for conversion rate optimization across all content and landing pages.

## Above-the-Fold Requirements

Every landing page must have above the fold (without scrolling):

1. **Clear headline** — specific value proposition, not clever or vague
2. **Supporting subheadline** — adds detail to headline, addresses "how" or "for whom"
3. **Primary CTA** — visible, prominent, action-oriented
4. **One trust signal** — social proof number, logo bar, or award

## CTA Standards

### Button Copy Rules
- Lead with a strong action verb: Start, Get, Join, Try, Download, See
- Be specific: "Start Free Trial" beats "Get Started"
- Reinforce value: "Get My Free Report" beats "Submit"
- First person works well: "Start My Free Trial"

### CTA Placement
- Above fold: always
- After each major section (long pages): at least 2-3 total
- Conclusion: always
- Sidebar or sticky (if applicable): consider for long-form content

### CTA Design Notes
- Contrasting color (different from rest of page)
- Sufficient size (minimum 44px height for mobile)
- White space around button — don't crowd it

## Trust Signals

### Required on Landing Pages
- [ ] Customer count or user number: "Join 10,000+ [customers]"
- [ ] Logo bar (recognizable brands using your product)
- [ ] At least 2 testimonials

### Testimonial Quality Standards
- Must be specific (mention result, not just praise)
- Must include: person's name, title, and company
- Better with: photo, metric result, before/after

**Good**: "We increased our [metric] by 40% in the first month using [product]. — Jane Smith, Marketing Director at Acme Corp"
**Bad**: "Great tool! Highly recommend. — John"

### Risk Reversals
- Free trial (no credit card required — say so explicitly)
- Money-back guarantee
- Cancel anytime messaging
- "No contracts" for SaaS

## Form Best Practices

- Minimum required fields only
- Don't ask for phone number unless essential
- Privacy assurance near submit: "We don't share your email"
- CTA replaces "Submit" — use specific action text
- Real-time validation (not just on submit)

## Page Structure for Landing Pages

```
1. Hero / Above-fold (headline, subhead, CTA, trust signal)
2. Problem (what pain does this solve?)
3. Solution (introduce your product/service as the answer)
4. Features/Benefits (3-6 key points, benefit-led)
5. Social Proof (testimonials, logos, numbers)
6. How It Works (simple 3-step process)
7. Objection Handling / FAQ
8. Final CTA (repeat or strengthen offer)
```

## Blog Post CTA Guidelines

- In-content CTA: mid-article (around 60-70% through), contextually relevant
- Conclusion CTA: always — link to related content, lead magnet, or product
- Sidebar CTA (if applicable): related resource or product trial
- Exit-intent (if applicable): valuable lead magnet offer

## A/B Testing Priorities

Test in this order (highest to lowest leverage):

1. Above-fold headline
2. CTA button copy
3. CTA button color/placement
4. Lead form field count
5. Social proof placement
6. Value proposition framing

## Conversion Metrics by Content Type

| Content Type | Primary Metric | Secondary Metric |
|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Landing page | Form conversion rate | CTA click rate |
| Blog post | Email opt-in rate | Time on page |
| Comparison page | Trial signup rate | Chat engagement |
| Product page | Demo request rate | Pricing page visits |
