# SEO Guidelines

Standards and requirements for all SEO-optimized content produced by SEO Machine.

## Content Length

| Content Type | Minimum | Target | Competitive+ |
|-------------|---------|--------|-------------|
| Blog post (informational) | 1,500 words | 2,000-2,500 words | 3,000+ words |
| Blog post (how-to/guide) | 2,000 words | 2,500-3,500 words | 4,000+ words |
| Landing page | 500 words | 800-1,200 words | 1,500+ words |
| Comparison/alternative page | 1,500 words | 2,000 words | 2,500+ words |

Actual target should be based on SERP competitor analysis — match or beat median competitor length.

## Keyword Optimization

### Primary Keyword
- **Density**: 1.0–2.0% (flag if < 0.5% or > 2.5%)
- **Placement requirements**:
  - [ ] H1 tag (exact or close variation)
  - [ ] First 100 words of content
  - [ ] At least 2 H2 subheadings
  - [ ] Meta title
  - [ ] Meta description
  - [ ] Conclusion/final section
  - [ ] URL slug (when possible)

### Secondary Keywords
- Each secondary keyword: 0.3–1.0% density
- Distributed naturally throughout relevant sections
- Do not force if context doesn't fit

### LSI/Semantic Keywords
- Include related terms, synonyms, and entity references
- Goal: demonstrate topical depth, not keyword stuffing
- Use tools like Google's People Also Ask for ideas

## Meta Elements

### Meta Title
- Length: **50-60 characters** (display-safe range)
- Include: primary keyword (ideally near the front)
- Include: brand name at end if character budget allows
- Format: `Primary Keyword: Benefit or Context | Brand`
- No duplicate titles across site

### Meta Description
- Length: **150-160 characters**
- Include: primary keyword naturally
- Include: value proposition or benefit
- Include: implied or explicit CTA
- No duplicate descriptions across site

### URL Slug
- Format: lowercase, hyphens, no underscores
- Include: primary keyword
- Short as possible while descriptive
- Avoid dates in URLs for evergreen content

## Heading Structure

```
H1: Article title (one per page, contains primary keyword)
  H2: Main section (2-3 H2s contain primary keyword variation)
    H3: Subsection (support H2, more specific)
      H4: Rarely needed, only for very long structured content
```

- H1: exactly one per page
- No skipping heading levels (H1 → H3 without H2)
- Each H2 should represent a distinct topic the reader expects
- H2s should cover primary subtopics from PAA and related searches

## Internal Linking

- **Minimum**: 3 internal links per article
- **Target**: 4-6 internal links per article
- **Anchor text**: descriptive, keyword-relevant, varied
- **Avoid**: "click here," "learn more," "this article"
- **Priority destinations**: pillar pages, high-converting pages, recent related content
- **Distribution**: spread throughout article, not all in one section
- **Never**: link to low-quality or thin content

## External Linking

- **Count**: 2-3 external links per article
- **Authority**: prefer .edu, .gov, established publications, original research
- **Recency**: statistics and data should be from last 3 years
- **Behavior**: open in new tab
- **Avoid**: linking to direct competitors without strategic reason

## Readability Requirements

- **Reading level**: 8th-10th grade (Flesch-Kincaid)
- **Flesch Reading Ease**: 60-70 (target)
- **Average sentence length**: 15-20 words
- **Paragraph length**: 2-4 sentences
- **Subheading frequency**: every 300-400 words
- **Lists**: use when 3+ parallel items would otherwise be long sentences
- **Passive voice**: < 15% of sentences

## Content Quality Standards

### Introduction (Required Elements)
1. Hook (question, stat, story, or bold claim in first 2 sentences)
2. Problem statement or context setup
3. Promise of what the reader will gain

### Body Content
- Actionable advice in every major section
- Evidence: data, examples, or case studies in every H2 section
- Answer the primary search intent fully
- Address secondary questions (PAA) within relevant sections
- Original perspective or unique angle vs. competitors

### Conclusion (Required Elements)
1. Key takeaway summary (brief — not a full re-read)
2. Call to action (relevant to content and business goal)

## Technical SEO Notes

- **Canonical**: use canonical tags for any duplicate or similar content
- **Schema markup**: add FAQPage schema for FAQ sections, HowTo for guides, Article for all posts
- **Image alt text**: descriptive, include keyword where natural, under 125 characters
- **Featured snippets**: use definition boxes, numbered steps, comparison tables to target position 0

## Content Update Policy

- Review top-performing content every 6 months
- Update any statistics older than 2 years
- Re-evaluate competitive positioning annually
- Flag content with declining rankings for rewrite consideration
